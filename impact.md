            <div class="col-lg-6 order-lg-1">
                <h3 class="section-heading">Scientific Impact</h3>
                <p>CloudSkin is advancing the scientific state-of-the-art in cloud-edge continuum computing by a cognitive, AI-driven orchestration and execution architecture across heterogeneous infrastructure. The key contribution is the realization of a cognitive computing continuum across heterogeneous cloud–edge environments including the following main innovations:</p>
                <ul>
                    <li class="mb-2"><strong>A novel Learning Plane:</strong> Uses AI and statistical decision making to dynamically manage and adapt resources allowing to optimize workloads, resources, energy, and network traffic in a “holistic” manner.</li>
                    <li class="mb-2"><strong>A Universal Execution Abstraction:</strong> Enables “stack identicality” across the continuum leveraging WebAssembly (Wasm) and Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) for near-native performance.</li>
                    <li class="mb-2"><strong>An Instrumented Storage Infrastructure:</strong> Provides real-time performance, adaptive data-tiering, and improved fault-tolerance driven by the Learning Plane.</li>
                </ul>
                <p><strong>Promoting Open Knowledge:</strong> CloudSkin is dedicated to open science, ensuring public access to its cloud-edge continuum platform and research outcomes.</p>
            </div>

</section>
