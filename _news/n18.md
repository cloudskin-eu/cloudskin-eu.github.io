Mathematical programming models have traditionally been overlooked in favor of heuristics
for solving the VM allocation problem, largely due to their perceived lack of scalability.
However, rather than using these models to directly solve the allocation problem, we proposed
a comprehensive VM allocation strategy that features two models, Micro and Macro, which
serve as composable building blocks within algorithms to effectively distribute the
computational workload.  <br/>
